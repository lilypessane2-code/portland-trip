Multnomah Falls Game Plan

parking, the lodge & the neighbors
Good news: no permit needed — the timed-permit season ended Sept 7 — and the falls are free.

How to get there

  • Fast way: I-84 East from Portland, ~30 miles / ~35 min → Exit 31 (Multnomah Falls).
  • Pretty way: the Historic Columbia River Hwy (through Troutdale & Corbett) — slower, and it's the road the nearby falls sit on. Use it for the way out or the stops after.
  • Going home: Tri-Cities is EAST on I-84 — leaving from the falls is on the way home, ~3 hrs, no backtracking.

Parking — best first

  • 1 · I-84 Exit 31 main lot — FREE, biggest. Paved path under the highway straight to the falls plaza. Aim here.
  • 2 · Lodge lot (Historic Hwy) — small, private, paid (up to ~$20), right across from the lodge. Take it only if a space is open — waiting isn't allowed when it's full.
  • If Exit 31 is closed/full: don't circle or use the shoulder — see Wahkeena or Latourell first and swing back.
  • Local tip: check the TripCheck camera for "Multnomah Falls Parking" before you leave Portland.

At the falls & the Lodge

  • Lower plaza — the full 620 ft two-tier view, no hiking needed.
  • Benson Bridge — the famous bridge between the tiers; ~5–10 min paved uphill. THE photo.
  • The Lodge (1925) — restaurant open Fri until 7 PM (falls-view room; smoked salmon chowder + pot roast are the favorites), gift shop, espresso bar, visitor center.
  • Skip today: the trail to the very top — steep, 2.4 miles round trip. The bridge gives you 90% of the magic.

Close by — pick 1–2

  • Wahkeena Falls — 5 min west (or a 0.5 mi trail from Multnomah): a splashy 242 ft cascade, totally different vibe.
  • Latourell Falls — ~10 min west on the Historic Hwy — its page — tall, thin, yellow-lichen cliff, short walk.
  • Bridal Veil Falls — quiet, short walk, between Latourell and Vista House.
  • Vista House at Crown Point — ~15 min west: the panoramic Gorge viewpoint, best wow-per-minute.

The afternoon, timed

  • ~12:45 leave Portland → ~1:20 park at Exit 31
  • 1:20–2:45 plaza, Benson Bridge, Lodge (coffee or early lunch + gift shop)
  • ~3:00 one nearby stop (Latourell or Vista House)
  • ~3:45–4:00 I-84 East → home ~7 PM
Sources: That Oregon Life — Multnomah Falls parking & permit guide (thatoregonlife.com) · Multnomah Falls Lodge (multnomahfallslodge.com) · US Forest Service, Columbia River Gorge NSA.

Multnomah Falls

the one you came for

Oregon's tallest waterfall — 620 feet in two drops — with the 1915 Benson Bridge standing in the spray. The postcard of Oregon.

PriceFree · Free in October
Who goesEveryone. First-timers, photographers, couples. It earns the hype.
WhereColumbia River Gorge
HoursAlways open · Lodge daytime hours

Good to know

  • I-84 Exit 31 · ~35 min east of Portland — and right on your drive route from Tri-Cities
  • 0.2-mile paved walk to Benson Bridge; the top trail is 1.2 miles of switchbacks (~90 min)
  • No timed permit and no fee in October — the $2 permit is summer only
  • Go Friday morning: softer light, emptier paths
  • The 1925 Lodge at the base has coffee, food and a gift shop
Fun factThe falls run year-round from underground springs on Larch Mountain — they never dry up, even in August.
